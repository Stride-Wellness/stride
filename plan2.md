# User Cases

Bob: An older adult who wants to use stride for himself.
  - Action: Downloads stride app.
  - Action: Fills out the questionair for himself, and makes an account as part of the same onboarding quiz (email + password or SSO)
  - Step: Make account for bob in database.
  - Action: Bob can now use that account.

John: A caregiver who works for Retirement Inc. (owned by Jessica) need to download stride so he can help his clients.
  - Action: Jessica clicks add employeed (she fills in his email, name, and role) and recieves a code which she sends to John.
  - Action: John downloads stride and puts his code in.
  - Step: John now has an account and is in the database assigned to to business.
  - Action: John can click add person (older adult, etc) which will register the person to the business and allow john to manage them.
  - Action: Jessica can also see an persons that john adds.

Company Inc: A company that wants to integrated stride to help their clients feel better, Ben owns the company.
  - Action: Ben goes on the website and clicks create get stride for my business. (for now we will do the setup)
  - Action: Ben will fill out his business contact info and billing info.
  - Step: Make bens account and business in the database.


Alice & Kate: A family member who wants to help her grandmother (Kate) fix her hip problem.
  - Action: Alice downloads stride app.
  - Action: Alice makes an observer account.
  - Step: Makes an account for Alice.
  - Action: Alice presses add client/family member fills out the questionair for Kate.
  - Step: Database registers Kate.
  - Action: Alice is presented with a few options for how to administer Stride to Kate: Share Code, Live Session, Email/SMS.
  - Action: Alice selects code and she is presented with a code that she can share to Kate.
  - Action: Kate downloads stride, puts in her code.

Kate, Alice, John, and Bob all only use the mobile app.
Kate and Bob only see the client view where they are being given excersises directly from stride.
Alice and John see the provider view where they can give excersises to their clients/family.

Jessice and Ben have a web dashboard to see their business.

# User Creation

When someone downloads the app, the first screen they see includes: 
  - A button for a client to create and use the account for themselves (Bob)
  - A button to put in a code (John and Kate)
  - A button for someone to create a provider account to help their family (Alice)

# Questionair

Bob: Base questionair + account info when signing up.
John and Kate: Account info is complete already they just need to enter code, no questions.
Alice: account info when signing up, base questionair for kate when adding her as a family member.
